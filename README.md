# Case File 11:59 (game only)

The murder mystery on its own. It ends on "Welcome to your 30s, Detective Kriti." with her detective rank and a Case Closed stamp. No photos, video, music or letter afterwards.

## Folder layout

```
index.html
style.css
script.js
robots.txt
lib/                    handwriting, quill and confetti scripts (leave as is)
photos/
    suspects/
        thorne.jpeg     Professor Cassius Thorne
        wren.jpeg       Wren Ashcroft
        crumb.jpeg      Bonnie Crumb
        odessa.jpeg     Odessa Vale
        kriti.jpeg      Detective Kriti
```

The game music and sound effects are generated by the page itself; no music file is needed.

## Changing the words

At the top of `script.js`, under `CONFIG`:

- `detective`: the detective's name
- `welcomeLine`: the line under "Welcome to your 30s, Detective Kriti."

## Testing

Progress saves in the browser. To start again, tap the red CLASSIFIED stamp on the cover 5 times, or tap "Replay the case" at the end.
